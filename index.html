About Chef Jay

Cooking with soul

James Thomas Garrett III, known as Chef Jay LeSoul, is a Cleveland-area private and celebrity chef who has enjoyed perfecting his craft for over 15 years.

After overcoming personal challenges, Chef Jay built a successful culinary career, and his journey of perseverance, recovery, and purpose was featured in a Seeds of Literacy Student Spotlight. Today, he provides private dining and catering services while leading a team and creating opportunities for others in his community.

His passion for food and community also inspired “Cooking in the Hood,” a concept Chef Jay created to celebrate where he comes from and show how food can connect, inspire, and uplift people in the community. Through it, he brings his culinary experience back to the neighborhood, using cooking not only to serve great food but also to encourage connection, mentorship, and opportunity.

For Chef Jay, cooking is more than preparing food. It’s about bringing people together, serving his community, creating opportunities, and always cooking with soul.
